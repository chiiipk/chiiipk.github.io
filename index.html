03 / About

Curiosity first, rigor always.

I am an undergraduate researcher in the Advanced Data Science and Artificial Intelligence program at Hanoi University of Science and Technology.

At the Foundation Model Lab, BKAI Center, I work on efficient and interpretable language modeling. I am especially interested in the structure hidden inside neural representations: how it forms, how it can be aligned across models, and how it can be transferred without carrying the full cost of a large model.
