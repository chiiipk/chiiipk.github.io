This project organizes VLM distillation methods along three views: the knowledge being transferred, teacher accessibility, and structural compatibility. It accompanies a survey accepted to Findings of EMNLP 2026 and maintains an open collection of papers and resources.

[Survey and resources](https://github.com/HoangTran223/Awesome-Knowledge-Distillation-of-VLMs)
