I am a Data Science and Artificial Intelligence student at [Hanoi University of Science and Technology](https://www.hust.edu.vn/) and a research member of the **Foundation Model Lab**. I work under the supervision of Dr. Ngo Van Linh on efficient language models and natural language processing.

**Research.** I study how to make large language models smaller and more efficient without losing their capabilities. My current work centers on **knowledge distillation**, **representation and trajectory alignment**, **cross-tokenizer transfer**, and **embedding model distillation**. I am also interested in reinforcement learning, optimal transport, and efficient multimodal models.

My recent work develops alignment-based objectives at multiple levels: layer-wise trajectories, tokenizer-agnostic spans, teacher-anchored layers, and time-warped sequences. I enjoy research that begins with a clear practical limitation, develops a natural technical idea, and evaluates it through careful implementation, ablation, and error analysis.

For more details, see my [publications](/publications/) or [CV](/cv/).
